Put your photographs in this folder.

Any .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif file dropped here appears on the site.
You do not need to resize or compress them first — the build makes
thumbnails, large versions, and blur placeholders for you.

To put a photo in a section, add its filename to that section's list in
src/data/photos.js. Photos you don't assign to a section still appear,
in an untitled section at the bottom.

Delete this file once you have added your own.
